# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS in a single `index.html` (existing project; no framework).

## Users

Food business owners and factory buyers in India and abroad who are comparing machinery suppliers. Their job: find the right machine or complete line for their product and capacity, then contact a supplier they can trust.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for Jas Enterprise, Ahmedabad, a manufacturer and exporter of food processing machinery since 1990. Success is a buyer sending an enquiry, calling a sales line, or messaging on WhatsApp.

## Positioning

A family-run Gujarat manufacturer (founder S. M. Sheth) that builds and customises its own machines, from single grinders to turnkey plants, and designs them so food keeps the texture and taste of hand-made.

## Operating Context

Buyers arrive from search or trade shows, often on a phone, and want to see the machine, know it exists in their category, and reach a person. Jas exhibits at trade shows (Kadhya Khurak 2026, Gandhinagar, 16–19 December 2026). Factory closed on Tuesdays.

- Enquiry form lives at jasenterprise.com/enquiry.html; this page links to it rather than collecting data.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Jas Enterprise". No visual identity is fixed; the user approved a free redesign and chose the Dyson style from styles.refero.design as the visual language.

## Evidence on Hand

- Product photographs hosted on jasenterprise.com (`/images/…`), hotlinked by the user's choice.
- ISO 9001:2015 certified; HACCP stated on the profile page.
- Exports to 75+ countries; 10,000+ clients (company's own claim on its profile page).
- Founder quote: "We do believe our success relies on our customer's success."
- No customer testimonials, logos or case studies are available; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Show the machine first; buyers decide from the object.
2. Every category is one tap from its real page.
3. A person is always reachable: phone, WhatsApp or enquiry, never hidden.
4. Only claims the company itself makes.
